---
title: Thinking
description: Trade speed for care on hard questions with the Think toggle.
---

Many current models can reason before they answer. Kakaw lets you choose how much, per message, with the **Think** button next to the message box.

| Think | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **On** | Hard questions: several tables, tricky filters, comparisons over time. Slower, often more accurate. |
| **Off** | Simple questions and slow models. Fastest replies. |

With Think off, every step of the answer keeps its reasoning short. With it on, the step that writes the SQL may reason at length; the smaller steps (checking the result, suggesting follow-ups, naming the chat) always stay short.

Think starts on. Your choice applies to every message you send until you reload the page. Models that don't reason ignore it.
