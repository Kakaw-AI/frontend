---
title: Stop, retry and your chats
description: Stop an answer, retry a failed one, find and delete your chats, and see your usage.
---

## Stop an answer

While an answer is being written, the send button turns into **Stop** (■). Stopping cancels the work on the server too, so no more model calls are made or billed. Text written so far stays, marked *You stopped this response*, with **Retry**.

## Retry

An answer that failed or was stopped shows **Retry**. It runs the same question again in the same place, without adding a new message.

## Chat titles

A new chat is named from its first question, in the language of the question, a moment after the answer arrives. Until then it shows *New chat*.

## Find your chats

The sidebar groups chats by database, newest first. Click a database's name to collapse or expand its chats. Each row shows the chat's title and when it was last used; a warning icon marks a chat whose model is no longer available.

## Your profile and usage

Choose your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open your profile. It shows how many chats, questions, databases and API keys you have, and the tokens your chats used: in total, in the last 30 days, and per model.

## Delete

- **A chat:** choose the bin icon on its row in the sidebar. It appears when you hover the row, and is always shown on touch screens. Its messages, results and charts are deleted.
- **A database:** open **Models** in the sidebar; the **Databases and models** window lists your databases with a delete button. Its chats, stored schema and remembered questions go with it, and an uploaded SQLite file is deleted from storage.
- **Your account:** open your profile and choose **Delete account**. It removes everything you own.
