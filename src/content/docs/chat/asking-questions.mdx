---
title: Asking questions
description: Write questions that get good answers, and build on them with follow-ups.
---

## Start a chat

Choose **New chat** in the sidebar, pick the database and the model above the message box, and type your question. **Enter** sends it; **Shift+Enter** starts a new line.

## Questions that work well

Say what you want to measure, over what, and for when:

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| *Show sales* | *Total sales per month in 2025* |
| *Best customers* | *Top 10 customers by total spent* |
| *Problems with orders* | *Orders that were cancelled last week, newest first* |

Use the words your data uses when you know them. Kakaw also sees your column names and sample values, so *"orders from APAC"* can still find a region stored as *Asia Pacific*.

## Follow-ups

Ask the next question as you would to a colleague. Kakaw reads the conversation, so these work:

- *Now only for Germany.*
- *What about last year?*
- *Chart that.*
- *Which of them ordered more than five times?*

Each follow-up builds on the SQL of the previous answer, not just its text, so filters carry over.

## When Kakaw asks back

If a question can be read more than one way, Kakaw asks which you mean and offers the choices as buttons. Pick one, or type your own answer.

## Suggested next questions

After an answer, up to three suggested follow-ups appear under it. Click one to send it.

## Any language

Ask in any language the model understands; the answer usually comes back in the same language. Arabic and other right-to-left text displays right to left, in your messages and in the answers.

## Questions beyond your data

When a question needs facts that aren't in your database, like an industry benchmark or someone's latest public release, Kakaw searches the web. See [Web search](/docs/web-search).
